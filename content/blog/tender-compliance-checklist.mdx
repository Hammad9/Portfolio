Tender compliance is where bids are won and lost before evaluation even begins. Use this checklist before every submission.

## Pre-Submission Checklist

- Read the entire tender document, including annexures and amendments.
- Confirm eligibility criteria and required experience.
- Prepare bid security in the correct format and amount.
- Verify document validity periods.
- Check file naming, format, and size requirements.
- Submit before the deadline with time to spare.

## Technical Proposal

- Address every requirement from the terms of reference.
- Include relevant experience, methodology, and team profiles.
- Use clear headings and page references.

## Financial Proposal

- Follow the pricing schedule exactly.
- Double-check arithmetic and totals.
- Include all applicable taxes and charges.

A compliant bid gets you a seat at the table. A compelling proposal wins the contract.
