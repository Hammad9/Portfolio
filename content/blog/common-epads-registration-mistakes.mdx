Pakistan's ePADS platform opens the door to public-sector contracts, but many vendors fail at the first step: registration. Here are the most common mistakes I see and how to avoid them.

## 1. Selecting the Wrong Vendor Category

Your category determines which tenders you can bid on. Choose categories that match your business registration, tax records, and actual capabilities. Mismatched categories lead to rejection or disqualification later.

## 2. Uploading Expired Documents

NTN, SECP registration, tax returns, and bank solvency certificates all have expiry dates. Check every document before upload and set calendar reminders for renewals.

## 3. Inconsistent Business Information

The name, address, and contact details on ePADS must match your official documents exactly. Small differences create verification delays.

## 4. Ignoring Profile Completeness

A partially completed profile limits your visibility and credibility. Fill every relevant field, upload all requested documents, and keep the profile updated.

## 5. Missing the Follow-Up

Registration approval is not instant. Monitor your email and portal notifications, and respond quickly to any requests for additional information.

## 6. Not Understanding the Procurement Rules

Registration is just the start. Spend time reading PPRA rules, tender eligibility criteria, and bid submission guidelines before bidding.

A clean, complete, and accurate ePADS profile is your foundation for public procurement success. Invest time upfront to avoid costly delays later.
